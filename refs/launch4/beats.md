# launch4.mp4: beats

Notion 3.7 release film. Source: 1920×1080, 30 fps, 66.41 s, −22.2 LUFS (quiet: the music sits about 8 LU under the usual −14 target).

**Evidence:**
- **Frames:** 2 fps frames in `refs/launch4/frames/`, where `f_N` is exactly (N − 1) × 0.5 s.
- **Transition strips:** 10 fps strips around every transition.
- **Transition timing:** the per-frame pixel change at full rate. A single-frame spike is a hard cut; a run of frames is a move.
- `~` marks a time read by eye (±0.1 s).
- On-screen copy is paraphrased, not transcribed.

**Palette** (`palette.json`):
- **Ground:** warm white `#faf9f8` (68 %) and charcoal `#191919` (21 %).
- **Accent:** one blue (`#2181df`) for carets, key words and buttons.
- Small amounts of tag colours (teal, ochre) appear inside UI only.

- One fictional project (a product launch with a fixed ship date and the same small team) runs through **every** feature: chat (12.0), meeting notes (13.0), search (13.5), plan (19.5), board (21–23), agents (23.5–27.4) and the final question (45–53).

- The cuts' nearest onsets are −160 to +160 ms away. Sync to the music is **not shown**.
